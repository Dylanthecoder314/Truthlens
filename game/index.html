How to play

You decide what people see

  1. Aim. Move your mouse (or drag on a touchscreen) to move the camera frame.
  2. Crop in or zoom out. Scroll, press - / +, or use the buttons. A tight crop hides the context. A wide shot shows it.
  3. Publish. Click, press Space, or tap the camera button. The crowd at the bottom reacts to your version.
  4. Watch the meters. Scary stories bring readers. Boring truths lose them. Run out of readers and someone louder takes over.

Every event can be told more than one way, and each choice leads somewhere different. There's no single right path, but there are consequences.
