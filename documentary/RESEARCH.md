# MH370 research notes

Fact sheet behind `index.html`, checked on 7 October 2026. Times are Malaysia Time (MYT, UTC+8) unless marked UTC.

## The flight

- Malaysia Airlines MH370 / callsign MAS370, Boeing 777-200ER, registration 9M-MRO, in service with the airline since 2002.
- Kuala Lumpur (KLIA) to Beijing. Scheduled arrival 06:30 MYT.
- 239 on board: 227 passengers (including 2 infants) and 12 Malaysian crew.
- Nationalities (final): China 153, Malaysia 50 (38 passengers + 12 crew), Indonesia 7, Australia 6, India 5, France 4, United States 3, Canada 2, Iran 2, New Zealand 2, Ukraine 2, Netherlands 1, Russia 1, Taiwan 1. Total 239. The two Iranians travelled on stolen Austrian and Italian passports, which is why early lists show those countries.
- Captain Zaharie Ahmad Shah, 53, joined Malaysia Airlines in 1981, more than 18,000 flying hours. First Officer Fariq Abdul Hamid, 27.

## Searches

| Phase | Dates | Area | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface search | Mar–Apr 2014 | Ocean surface | Multinational, Australia-led. Ocean Shield acoustic detections later ruled out. |
| Underwater (ATSB) | Oct 2014 – 17 Jan 2017 | >120,000 km² | Funded by Malaysia, Australia, China. About A$200 million. Depths up to ~6 km. |
| Ocean Infinity I | 22 Jan – late May 2018 | >112,000 km² | No find, no fee. Seabed Constructor with AUVs. Ended 29–30 May 2018. |
| Ocean Infinity II | 25 Feb 2025 – 23 Jan 2026 | ~7,571 km² | Contract signed 25 Mar 2025; halted April 2025 (weather); resumed 30 Dec 2025. 28 search days. Target area 15,000 km². No find announced 8 Mar 2026. |
| Extension | 1 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2027 | 7,428.54 km² remaining | Cabinet approved June 2026. US$70 million only on a find. |

## Debris

Of 27 items examined for the 2018 Safety Investigation Report: 3 confirmed, 19 in total rated confirmed, almost certain or highly likely, 1 likely, 7 unidentifiable.

| Part | Where | Found | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Right flaperon | Saint-André, Réunion | 29 Jul 2015 | Confirmed |
| Engine cowling fragment (Rolls-Royce stencil) | Mossel Bay, South Africa | Dec 2015 | Almost certain |
| Horizontal stabiliser panel ("NO STEP") | Sandbank off Vilankulo, Mozambique | Feb 2016 | Almost certain |
| Left outboard flap section | Mauritius | May 2016 (confirmed Oct 2016) | Confirmed |
| Right outboard flap section | Pemba Island, Tanzania | Jun 2016 (confirmed Sep 2016) | Confirmed |

CSIRO drift modelling pointed to the 7th arc, most likely north of the 2014–17 search area. Flap analysis suggests flaps were retracted at impact.

## 2018 Safety Investigation Report (30 July 2018)

- Could not determine the cause.
- Course changes likely made under manual control.
- No evidence of abnormal behaviour or stress in either pilot.
- Could not rule out unlawful interference by a third party.
- Air traffic control lapses: slow to initiate emergency phases, radar not monitored continuously, reliance on airline information, military not contacted.

## Families and courts

- Next-of-kin group Voice370.
- December 2025: Beijing court ordered Malaysia Airlines to pay about 2.9 million yuan to each of eight families; 23 cases pending; 47 settled and withdrawn.

## Approximations on the page

- **Fig. 1 track.** Fixes used (lon, lat): KLIA 101.710, 2.745 · IGARI 103.585, 6.936 · Kota Bharu 102.29, 6.17 · south of Penang 100.28, 5.22 · Pulau Perak 98.94, 5.68 · last radar 96.72, 6.81 (approximate). Positions between fixes are linear interpolations. The planned route beyond IGARI is schematic.
- **Fig. 2 rings.** Small circles around the sub-satellite point (0°, 64.5°E). Radii were derived from the published BTO values (18:25 12,520 µs; 19:41 11,500; 20:41 11,740; 21:41 12,780; 22:41 14,540; 00:11 18,040; 00:19 18,400), anchored so the 7th arc passes through the 2014–17 search area. That gives 32.9°, 30.6°, 31.2°, 33.5°, 37.1°, 43.6° and 44.2°. Checks: KLIA on the ground (14,820 µs) maps to 37.6° against a true 37.3°, and the 18:25 ring passes within a few km of the last radar fix. Satellite inclination and aircraft altitude are ignored.
- **Fig. 2 search areas.** Drawn as bands along the 7th arc: 2014–17 from 39.5°S to 33°S, 2018 from 35.5°S to 25°S. Widths are chosen to match the published areas. The real boundaries were irregular.
